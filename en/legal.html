What Masar Oman is

Masar Oman is a free digital travel guide. It is not a government body. It does not represent the Ministry of Heritage and Tourism, the Environment Authority or any other official entity, and it does not sell bookings.

When we cite a decree, a decision or an official website, it is only to show the source. It does not imply a partnership or an endorsement. We do not use official logos.

The listings of companies and guides are for guidance, not recommendations. "Listed in the official register" means the business appeared in a list published on an official portal on the verification date shown next to it. We never call a business "licensed" or "certified" without a source.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking or analytics tools. We do not collect your name, email or browsing data.
  • Stored on your device only: your trip, saved places and plans, language, light or dark mode and map base layer. They stay in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us. You can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Your location: requested only when you tap a feature such as "Nearest to me" or "My location", and only with your browser's permission. It is used to calculate distances and show what is around you. The coordinates may be sent to the open map services listed below to fetch nearby services or draw a route.
  • "List your business" and "Report wrong information" forms: not active yet. They send nothing and say so. When they are switched on, this page will be updated with what is collected, where it is stored and for how long. Licence documents uploaded then will never be published; only the reviewer will see them.
  • Third-party services your browser contacts directly when showing their content. Each has its own privacy policy:
    • Wikimedia Commons (photos)
    • Esri and OpenStreetMap (base maps)
    • Open-Meteo (weather)
    • Overpass (road services)
    • OSRM (routing)
    • Google Fonts (fonts)
    • unpkg (the Leaflet map library)
    • 360cities and Google Maps (360° tours and Street View)

Terms of use

  • The information is general guidance. Conditions, opening times, fees, permits and road status can change without notice. Check with the official authority before you travel.
  • We show the source and last-verified date where available. When something has not been verified, we say so.
  • We do not guarantee the accuracy of third-party data such as OpenStreetMap or weather services. We are not responsible for the content or services of websites we link to.
  • We do not show ratings, visitor numbers or customer testimonials. There is no review system in place.
  • By using the site you accept these terms. We may update them; the date at the top of this page shows the latest change.

Photos and copyright

  • Place photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences such as CC BY and CC BY-SA. Each one is credited with the photographer's name and its licence. The full list is on the photo credits page.
  • We do not use generated images or photos of other places. If no licensed photo exists for a place, it is shown without one. When a photo shows the area rather than the exact spot, the caption says so.
  • If you own a photo and see a wrong credit or licence, tell us through the contact channel and we will fix or remove it.

Correcting information

  • We rely on published sources: the Official Gazette, the Environment Authority, the Experience Oman portal, OpenStreetMap and Wikimedia. We show the source and the last-verified date.
  • If you find wrong or outdated information, report it, ideally with a link to the correct source. We review every report. We either correct the information and update the verification date, or explain why it stays.
  • Company and guide details we have not verified are marked "under verification" or shown without a badge. We never show a phone number or booking link we have not verified.

Adventures and trails disclaimer

  • Mountain, sea, wadi and desert activities carry real risks. You are responsible for your own decisions, fitness and equipment. Trail descriptions and difficulty levels are estimates and may not match conditions on the day you go.
  • Do not enter wadis or camp in a wadi bed when rain is possible: flash floods can arrive suddenly from rain far away. Check Oman Meteorology before you set out.
  • Avoid hiking in the heat of the day, carry enough water, tell someone your route and return time, and do not go out on difficult trails alone.
  • For specialised sea and mountain activities, use an operator licensed by the relevant authority, and check the licence yourself.
  • In an emergency call 9999.

Contact

For corrections, suggestions and photo requests: open an issue on GitHub. An on-site contact form will be switched on later.
