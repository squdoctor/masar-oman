Western Hajar Starlight Reserve

محمية الحجر الغربي لأضواء النجوم

The highest reserve in eastern Arabia, created to protect dark skies. Winter nights are clear and cold — bring warm layers.

Type
Nature reserve
Governorate
Al Dakhiliyah
Wilayat
Al Hamra — Jebel Shams
Visit status
Open to visitors
Legal basis
Royal Decree 40/2019 (2019)
Area
386 km²
Notable species
Arabian tahr, Juniper woodland, Bonelli’s eagle
Permit
No permit needed to walk the marked trails. The last stretch of road to the plateau needs a 4x4.
