Arabian Oryx Reserve

محمية المها العربية

The Jiddat al Harasis plain, where the Arabian oryx was returned to the wild after going extinct there. A long trip that needs a 4x4 and planning.

Type
Nature reserve
Governorate
Al Wusta
Wilayat
Haima
Visit status
By permit
Legal basis
Royal Decree 4/94 (1994)
Area
2824 km²
Notable species
Arabian oryx, Arabian gazelle, Nubian ibex, Arabian wolf
Permit
Entry, touring, hiking and camping need a written Environment Authority permit (Decision 11/2026). There is no public visitor gate; arrange visits in advance.
