Daymaniyat Islands Nature Reserve

محمية جزر الديمانيات الطبيعية

Nine low islands about 18 km offshore. You reach them on a licensed boat to snorkel over coral among turtles, and you’re back before sunset.

Type
Marine reserve
Governorate
Muscat
Wilayat
Seeb, off Barka
Visit status
By permit
Legal basis
Royal Decree 23/96 (1996)
Area
203 km²
Notable species
Coral reefs, Green turtle, Hawksbill turtle, Seabirds
Permit
A paid entry permit is bought online from the Environment Authority; operators often include it in the trip price. Landing and camping on the islands are closed May to October.
Coordinates
23.8334, 58.0584
