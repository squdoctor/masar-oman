Khor Al Baleed Reserve

محمية خور البليد

Inside Al Baleed Archaeological Park, part of the Land of Frankincense (UNESCO). One of Dhofar’s nine khawrs protected since 1997.

Type
Wetland
Governorate
Dhofar
Wilayat

Visit status
By permit
Legal basis
Royal Decree 49/97 (1997)
Permit
Since 26 October 2026, entry to the Salalah-coast khawr reserves needs an Environment Authority permit (Decision 193/2026). Fees: OMR 1 Omani, OMR 3 non-Omani, 100 baisa under 18. School trips and residents are exempt.
