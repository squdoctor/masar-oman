Al Qurm Nature Reserve

محمية القرم الطبيعية

A small mangrove forest in the heart of Muscat and Oman’s first Ramsar site (2013). Best for birds early in the morning at low tide.

Type
Wetland
Governorate
Muscat
Wilayat
Bawshar
Visit status
Open to visitors
Legal basis
Royal Decree 38/75 (1975)
Area
1.72 km²
Notable species
Grey mangrove, Flamingo, Herons, Mud crabs
Permit
No permit needed to watch from the adjoining park and beach. Entering the mangrove itself is not allowed.
